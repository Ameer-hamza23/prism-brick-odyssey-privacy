Privacy Policy for Prism Brick Odyssey

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

Developer: Zenith Stack Studio

Application: Prism Brick Odyssey (com.zenithstack.prismbrickodyssey)

1. Introduction

Zenith Stack Studio is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application Prism Brick Odyssey.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Prism Brick Odyssey is designed from the ground up as an offline-first casual puzzle game. We do not require account registration to play.

  • Local Gameplay Data: Your level progress, high scores, ball unlock states, and game preferences are saved locally on your device.
  • Advertising: The game may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), general device information, and coarse location data to serve personalized or contextual advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.
  • Optional Cloud Synchronization: If cloud backup features are enabled, your progress snapshot is encrypted and associated solely with your authorized cloud identifier.

3. Children's Privacy

Prism Brick Odyssey complies with the Family Policy requirements and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

4. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data. Local game saves are stored within the application's sandboxed private storage. Network transmissions for ads or optional leaderboards use standard TLS 1.3 encryption.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
